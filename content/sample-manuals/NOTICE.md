Sample assembly manuals in this folder:

- `kallax.pdf` — official IKEA KALLAX assembly instructions published by Inter IKEA Systems B.V. for customers.
- `lack.pdf`, `bekvam.pdf` — assembly documents from the IKEA 3D Assembly Dataset (Inter IKEA Systems B.V.), shared to support assembly-instruction research.

They are sample inputs only. IKEA remains the rights holder. Do not treat this repo as a redistribution of IKEA product designs.
